The Concept

Old soul.
Real Dubai.

Retro-restomod 4x4s, reborn with real Dubai soul. We take the Suzuki Jimny and give it a chrome-faced, body-color, hand-finished 70s revival — built for the Dubai you actually live in. The Jumeirah 1 is our 2-door, the first of the range.

Cali Motors is a homegrown UAE marque. We start with the humble, go-anywhere Jimny and rebuild the face in polished chrome — a horizontal-slat grille, round lamps, a vintage bumper — then body-color the flares, roll it on chrome smoothie wheels, and finish it by hand. The result is a retro icon that feels equally at home outside a Satwa cafeteria, in the Alserkal courtyard, or halfway up a dune.
